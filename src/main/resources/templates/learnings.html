What human reviewers have taught menD

Every rejection and every significant review comment is handed back to the session that wrote the code, and then mined for a lesson. Repository lessons are injected into that repository's future scoping and remediation sessions. General lessons are true beyond one codebase, so menD recommends promoting them somewhere they outlive menD — a Devin knowledge note, the repository's own instruction file, or menD's own backlog. Lessons that keep being followed by review feedback are retired rather than accumulated.
